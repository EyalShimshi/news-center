        Keyboard shortcuts

        1–9, ← →
        Select a channel (switches the fullscreen channel too)
        M
        Mute or unmute the selected channel
        S
        Solo the selected channel; solo several to hear all of them
        Shift+S, Ctrl+click S
        Solo only this channel
        Enter, the button in the middle of a channel
        Spotlight the channel and hear only it; every other channel is muted. Again to go back to the grid.
        ↑ ↓
        Selected channel volume, up to 200%
        F, double-click
        Fill the window with the selected channel
        D,
        Open the selected channel in its own window, to put anywhere: another monitor, or on top of other apps. Its sound follows the mixer here. In that window, F or a double-click fills its screen, T keeps it on top, and D or closing it brings the channel back.
        P
        Spotlight the selected channel
        G
        Switch between Grid and Spotlight
        Drag a channel
        Move it to another place; drop it on the big spotlight channel to swap them
        + −, Ctrl+wheel
        Bigger or smaller tiles: smaller ones sit side by side, bigger ones scroll
        0
        Fit all tiles on screen
        V, Shift+V
        Hide or show the level meter on the selected channel, or on all of them
        R
        Reload the selected channel
        N
        Show or hide the news
        K
        Open or close the Knesset view (in Hebrew): today's plenum and committee sessions, committees, votes, legislation, MKs, the government and a guide. F5 refreshes it, Esc closes it.
        F5
        Refresh everything and look for new Knesset streams
        C
        Open or close the channel manager
        Esc
        Leave fullscreen, close panels and dialogs

        Every channel starts muted. S also unmutes the channel, and when any channel is soloed only the soloed channels are heard. Mute always wins. Double-click a volume slider to reset it to 100%.
