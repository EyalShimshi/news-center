# Privacy

News Center (for Android and for Windows) has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It
doesn't collect, sell or share personal data.

## What it connects to

- **Broadcasters**: the live streams and pages of the channels you have switched on (Kan, Keshet /
  Mako, Reshet 13, Channel 14, Channel 16, TV10, i24NEWS, the Knesset, Channel 9, and any channel
  you add yourself). Like any video player, it sends them your IP address and a browser-style
  User-Agent. Channel 12 is Mako's own web page and player, which may use its own cookies and
  measurement, as it does in a browser.
- **knesset.tv**: its live page and committee pages, to find live Knesset broadcasts.
- **The Knesset's data services** (knesset.gov.il): its open data and its website's own data
  service, while the Knesset view (Windows) or tab (Android) is open, and its photos of MKs and
  committees. Recordings you play stream from the Knesset's video servers. Links to the Knesset's
  pages and documents open in your browser.
- **News sites**: the RSS and Atom feeds of the news sources you have switched on, while the News
  tab (Android) or panel (Windows) is open. Tapping a headline opens the article in your browser.
- **GitHub**: only when you tap a link to it (source code, updates, privacy, reporting a problem).

## What stays on your device

Your channel list and order, news sources, settings, and the Knesset view's last tab. On Android, if the app crashes, the
details of the last crash are kept on the device so that you can copy them or send them as a
GitHub issue; nothing is sent automatically.

## Permissions (Android)

- **Internet** and **network state**: to play streams and load news.

That's all: no location, contacts, storage, microphone or notifications.
