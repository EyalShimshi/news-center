## News Center 1.1.0

The Windows app gets a **Knesset view**; both apps stop using one news site.

### Windows (`news-center-windows-v1.1.0.zip`)

- **Knesset** in the title bar (or `K`): a full-window view, in Hebrew, of the Knesset's
  day-to-day work, straight from the Knesset's own data:
  - **היום** – what's on air now, today's plenum and committee sessions with their agendas, the
    next plenum sitting, the week ahead in the committees.
  - **ועדות** – every committee with its chair, responsibilities, members, staff, sessions and press
    releases.
  - **מליאה** and **הצבעות** – the sittings and their agendas, every vote and how each MK voted.
  - **חקיקה** – every bill with its stage in plain words, and recently passed laws with summaries.
  - **חברי הכנסת** and **ממשלה** – the factions, all 120 MKs (who's in the building now), the
    government.
  - **מדריך** – how the Knesset works, how a law is made, a glossary, and links to the Knesset's
    pages.
  - Live sessions open in the grid as the big channel; recordings play in place.
- One news site was removed from the sources and is blocked everywhere in the window.

Unzip and run `NewsCenter.exe` (Windows 10 or 11). See
[DESKTOP.md](https://github.com/EyalShimshi/news-center/blob/main/DESKTOP.md).

### Android (`news-center-v1.1.0.apk`, Android 12 or newer)

- One news site was removed from the sources and is blocked: no feed, channel, link or web page
  request goes to it.

Install over 1.0.0. `mapping-v1.1.0.zip` decodes Android crash reports from this version.
